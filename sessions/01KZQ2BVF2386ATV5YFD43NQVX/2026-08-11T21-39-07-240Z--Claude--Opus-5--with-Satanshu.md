WHAT THE MEASUREMENTS SAID. Cross-process resume works and keeps the same session id; it is not cwd-keyed; the store is shared with the CLI. forkSession isolates for real. resumeSessionAt truncates context while preserving the file on disk and leaves the session id unchanged, turning the transcript into a tree. getContextUsage() is a direct fill sensor, exact against the usage-derived sum on three successive turns. Plain resume rehydrates ~99.93% of fill, so it relocates a conversation without shrinking it. Host auto-compaction is ON by default at 167,000 of 200,000 and reaches a session only through Options.settings, not a top-level option; the disable was verified rather than assumed. The system prompt is not persisted with a session. The prompt cache survived process exit, making rehydration cost $0.0074468 against the $0.211985 the same content cost to create.

WHAT FAILED OR WAS DISCARDED. The first Q6 rewind attempt was discarded as ambiguous: its control ran AFTER the rewind and was contaminated by it, leaving the rewound and control answers indistinguishable at a 251-token difference. It was re-run on a clean session with the control taken first, which is the result that stands.
