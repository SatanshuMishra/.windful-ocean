# 0595. The stack is frozen at twenty-four units and the architect is retired

## Context

The stack grew 16 units to 21 to 22 while four shipped. The growth had one mechanism, and it was a process error by the orchestrator rather than an inevitability: every blocked lead was routed to the architect, and an architect asked to resolve a block returns design, which is more units. Four of the five additions came from that loop. The user directed that scope be frozen now and the work pushed through.

## Options

- Keep routing blocks to the architect and accept that each block can add units
- Freeze the unit list, retire the architect, and rule blocks at the orchestrator in one exchange
- Drop the filed findings entirely to stop the growth

## Outcome

FROZEN at twenty-four units, taken from the wave map's own enumeration rather than its stated total of twenty-two, because an enumeration that disagrees with its total may hide a missing unit and the list is the safer reading. Wave 1 M0, M1, M10, M12a, M14; wave 2 M2, M7, M12b-1; wave 3 M3, M4, M4b, M12b-2, M12c; wave 4 M8a; wave 5 M5, M8b, M9, M12b-3; wave 6 M6; wave 7 M11, M13; wave 8 M17 then M15; wave 9 M16. FOUR BINDING RULES, which override any earlier guidance to a lead. One: the architect is retired and no lead escalates to it; a block comes to the orchestrator with a recommended answer and is ruled in ONE exchange. Two: a block the orchestrator cannot rule in one exchange does not wait - the unit ships what it can with an honest downgrade and the remainder goes to the backlog. Three: nothing discovered from here becomes a unit; it is one line in BACKLOG-not-this-stack.md, which is read only after the stack ships. Four: one reviewer and one round per unit, a second round only on a reviewer BLOCK, and no separate verifier pass. This is the receipts doctrine applied to the stack rather than to a single unit - acceptance is a ceiling and discovery above it is filed, never folded - and the defect was that filed items were flowing back into the same stack instead of into a backlog. Deleting the findings was rejected because the growth was never caused by recording them.
