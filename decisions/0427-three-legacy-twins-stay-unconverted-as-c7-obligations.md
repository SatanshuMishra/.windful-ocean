# 0427. The three legacy twins stay unconverted as named C7 obligations

## Context

C4a's exec chokepoint does not reach three legacy call paths: run-engine.mjs at two sites and divergence.mjs. Converting them inside C4a would have widened an MSP already carrying a six-invariant fix round across three proven force bypasses.

## Options

- Convert them inside C4a - rejected: widens an MSP already at the edge of reviewable size
- Leave them silently - rejected: an unnamed gap is indistinguishable from an unnoticed one
- Leave them unconverted but named as C7 obligations - chosen

## Outcome

The three twins stay unconverted and are named as C7 obligations in the verdict, so the gap is recorded rather than discovered later. With the porting MSP now inserted before C7 by decision 0424, these obligations drain there rather than into C7 itself. Naming a gap in the verdict is the discipline that separates a deferred item from an overlooked one.
