# 0373. Where mitosis conflicts with fanout the conflicting element changes; six resolutions are binding

## Context

The user directed that fanout recommendations win any conflict, and that the conflicting element be changed to work WITH fanout rather than fanout being bent to fit. Six genuine conflicts were found between fanout's design and mitosis as it stands.

## Options

- Adopt fanout selectively where cheap and keep mitosis's shape elsewhere - rejected by user directive
- Change every conflicting mitosis element to work with fanout - chosen
- Copy fanout's code - rejected: PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 (LICENSE.md:31-41) makes a verbatim lift into a public repo a licensing decision, and the architecture is what transfers, not the lines

## Outcome

Six binding resolutions. (1) The unmerged spec's law 1, prose to typed verb NEVER dispatch elimination, is rewritten: elimination IS the goal for mechanical work and prose stays the ABI only for the 9 judgment kinds. (2) 0353 superseded per 0370. (3) wave-planner's barriers become a ready_after DAG - fanout tests that a DAG beats a barrier chain (fanout.py:246-255); waves survive as a diagnostic only. (4) derive-edges stops deciding coupling silently and emits {pair, signals, default}, with an unrendered verdict a hard stop, same family as 0343. (5) .mitosis/run.json genesis overwrite (mitosis.js:4268) becomes a content-keyed run dir mirroring .fanout/plan_id. (6) fileScope splits into edit-set and read-set with truncation reported never hidden. Three fanout defects are explicitly NOT inherited: success-as-exit-0 (we keep schemas), no run lock (we take O_EXCL), and the resume hole where task prose is not hashed (our key covers it).
