# 0610. Strike c5 rather than rewrite it to pass: the measurement is complete and the bar is failed

## Context

c5 held three conjuncts. Attribution is met. The closed census halting on the unclassifiable is met, and after round 2 it is genuinely met rather than inherited. The third, Lead share clearing its pinned bar, is decided negative at 24.23 to 26.60 percent against 50 percent, at n of 421, produced end to end by the repaired instrument. ## Options

- Rewrite c5 to describe what was achieved, which would make it pass
- Strike c5, retaining it, with the negative result recorded
- Leave c5 open indefinitely against an unsatisfiable criterion

## Outcome

STRIKE c5. Rewriting it to describe what was achieved would make a failed criterion pass by editing the criterion, which is the exact reward hack the acceptance-as-a-ceiling rule exists to prevent, and it would erase the only durable record that the delegation shape the rebuild targeted did not materialise. A struck criterion is retained, never deleted, so the negative survives in the ledger alongside the decisions that produced it. Leaving it open is refused too: work against an unsatisfiable criterion cannot terminate, and the measurement it demanded is complete. The separate question of whether a greater-than-50-percent Lead-share bar ever encoded the right target opens as its OWN thread on its own evidence. It is explicitly NOT folded back here, because re-examining a bar after seeing the number that failed it is post-hoc grading, and c5's outcome stands whatever that examination concludes.
