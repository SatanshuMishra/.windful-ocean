# 0450. Redispatch stays a judgment kind and the six mechanical remediation attachments are deleted

## Context

SPEC residual 7 requires C7 to give redispatch a determinate home, because its nature is fixed by the triggering stage rather than by the call site, and an unclassifiable entry is exactly what a closed census exists to halt on. Reconnaissance found the premise partly obsolete: redispatch is ALREADY a registered prompt kind (prompt-contract.mjs:36, :235-243; prompt-registry.mjs:36; prompt-remediate.mjs:77-87), pinned by the closed census at tests/prompt-contract.test.mjs:25 and two frozen fixtures. It also found SPEC section 2.3's row at :138 factually wrong: it pins redispatch to debugger/opus, while mitosis.js:3779 dispatches it with the triggering stage's agentType and schema and :3756 derives the model from the stage. Ten makeRemediation attachment sites exist; six sit on stages the section 1.1 census calls mechanical (mitosis.js:4184, :4803, :4975, :5122, :5219, :5246), which become spawnSync git calls after conversion, and four on judgment stages (:1593, :4396, :5047, :5090).

## Options

- Option (a) from residual 7: the retry becomes a property of each converted stage. Costs deleting a hardened fixture-frozen contract entry, editing the closed census and two frozen fixtures, and re-authoring four bespoke correction shapes, prompts and fixture pairs.
- Option (b) from residual 7: redispatch survives as a registered judgment kind. Zero net-new registration, contract, prompt or fixture cost, because all of it already exists and is pinned.
- Leave it unclassified. Refused by the closed-census rule.

## Outcome

Option (b), with the six mechanical attachments detached rather than ported. Once C7 converts those six stages to deterministic code, nothing in the mechanical column can trigger a model dispatch, so redispatch becomes reachable only from the four judgment stages and is determinately a judgment kind at zero registration cost. The six deletions are a forced consequence of the conversion C7 already performs, not a second design; ci-to-green's stage-owned redispatch at mitosis.js:5501 stays stage-owned. C7 must also correct SPEC :54 and :138. Falsifier: find one of the six converted mechanical stages whose post-conversion failure mode has no code-expressible correction and still needs a model to recover; that would put redispatch back across both columns and make option (a) mandatory.
