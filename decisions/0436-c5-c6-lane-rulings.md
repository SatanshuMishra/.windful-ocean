# 0436. C5 splits and ships the eighth verb, C6 does not extend the CI matrix, and skills byte-cleanliness holds to D1

## Context

Three questions blocked cutting the C5 and C6 lanes. The SPEC was read directly rather than recalled. C5's Files list is the engine Parallelize path alone and its acceptance is fixture-based, so the SPEC does not call for a gate verb -- and the SPEC is explicit when it wants one, since A4 names all three verbs, their three registration points and a red case each. Against that: the coupling verdict is not merely unarmed but decorative. deriveEdges converts no coupling decision into anything, two file-disjoint tasks under one directory come back with zero added edges even when --verdicts explicitly says serialize, and grep -c coupling against mitosis.js returns 0. C6's file is named boundary-gate.mjs but is a boundary-validation program, not a mitosis-gate verb. C5 is required to audit plan-to-task-graph/SKILL.md, and every MSP from C1 through C4c has held .claude/skills/ byte-clean.

## Options

- Follow the SPEC literally on all three: no verb in C5, no matrix extension in C6, and leave the skills question to whoever hits it
- Split C5 into C5a enforcement and C5b coupling-parity verb so the eighth verb ships, hold C6 to its five behavioral cases, and hold skills byte-cleanliness to D1
- Ship the verb inside a single undivided C5, accepting a roughly 780 LOC MSP

## Outcome

Option 2. The verb ships because fixing only the arming would deliver a louder inert guard, and because every MSP from C1 through C4c ships a verb that C7 is designed to trust -- a producer fix with no receipt breaks that chain exactly where the evidence says the chain is already fake. It ships as C5b so neither half exceeds the review target. C6 takes the SPEC's five behavioral acceptance cases and registers nothing in MITOSIS_GATE_VERBS, CI_ENFORCER_CHECK_TOKENS or receipts.yml; the word gate in its filename is a naming trap. C5 audits the SKILL.md by reading and reporting it in the PR body, and writes nothing under .claude/skills/, since D1 is the designed write point. Recorded in full at RULINGS-2026-08-14-C5-C6.md. Note explicitly: the C5 split is a quality decision, not a speed one -- review is a flat per-MSP cost, so splitting multiplies it.
