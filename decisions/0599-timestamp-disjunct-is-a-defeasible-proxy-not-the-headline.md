# 0599. A pre-registered rule's timestamp proxy loses to direct falsification of the headline it proxies for

## Context

The pre-registered rule made REUSE sufficient on R2, start rows in one group describing different work, and listed widely separated non-overlapping time spans among R2's proxies. That disjunct FIRED: 214 in-group gaps exceed 60 seconds. The investigator surfaced the deviation and handed the judgment back rather than silently resolving it. Honouring the proxy literally would have returned REUSE; honouring the headline returned DUPLICATION.

## Options

- Honour the fired proxy literally and rule REUSE
- Rule DUPLICATION because the headline the proxy stands for is directly falsified
- Declare the rule broken and return inconclusive

## Outcome

DUPLICATION. R2's four items are proxies for R2's own headline, and that headline is directly falsified: one toolUseId, one description, one transcript and one sidecar per group. The rule's own MIXED clause says corpus evidence outranks an INFERENCE about the mechanism, never a directly read assignment traced to the field, and _observer.mjs:75 is the latter. The real defect is named rather than argued past: the pre-registration anticipated two mechanisms and reality holds three, so re-entry was unrepresented in it. Anti-convenience check on the ruling: it selects NEITHER pinned reading. Group grain yields a third figure nobody held, and re-entry specifically over-counts delivery-lead at row grain, so the settled number had to land below 60.74 percent. The ruling produced no comfortable answer, which is the evidence that it was not graded to fit.
